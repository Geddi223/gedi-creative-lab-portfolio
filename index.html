About Me

Learn more about my skills, experience, and creative process

Video Editor, Motion Designer & Graphic Designer

Hi, I'm Gedion Fikre, a Video Editor and Motion Graphics Designer from Wolaita Sodo, South Ethiopia, with a BSc in Information Systems from Haramaya University (graduated June 2025). I combine technical problem-solving with visual storytelling, animation and compositing.

I use Adobe Premiere Pro, After Effects and Photoshop to produce digital video content, motion graphics, visual effects and online media projects, with careful attention to pacing, detail and final delivery.

Through Gedi Creative Lab I manage projects from concept and planning to final export, using transitions, effects, sound design, color adjustments and animated titles to keep viewers engaged.
